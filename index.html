Frequently asked questions

Is ResumeMakery really free?

Yes. The current product has no paid plan. Resume creation, templates, ATS tools and PDF downloads are free.

Can I edit my existing resume?

Yes. Import a supported PDF, DOCX, TXT file or resume image, review the extracted content and continue editing it in structured fields.

Does an ATS score guarantee an interview?

No. ResumeMakery provides practical, deterministic guidance. Employers use different systems and human judgment, so no score can guarantee an interview or hiring outcome.

Do I need an account to start?

No. You can build your first resume and download the first PDF as a guest. A free account is used to save your work and continue later.
