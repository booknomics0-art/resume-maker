## 3. How to Use in CraftCV

All 60 templates are fully integrated into the CraftCV resume engine:
- In the **Design Step (Step 7)** or **Template Gallery**, use the filter chips:
  - Click **Resume.io (36)** to see only Resume.io templates.
  - Click **Canva Styles (24)** to see only Canva templates.
  - Click **All (165)** to browse the entire collection.
- Every template renders your live resume data into an exact A4 sheet.
- 100% Free: Click **Download PDF** to export without any watermark or subscription.
