Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Plain-language summary: ResumeMakery needs some data to build, save and improve resumes. Guest drafts are temporary. Signed-in users can save resume data to their account. We use web analytics to understand site usage. We do not sell resume content or personal data to advertisers.

1. Who we are

ResumeMakery is a browser-based resume builder operated from India. Questions about this policy, access requests or deletion requests can be sent to privacy@resumemakery.com. General product support is available at support@resumemakery.com.

2. Information you provide

Account information

If you create an account, we process the information needed to authenticate and identify the account, such as your name, email address, authentication provider and account identifiers. Password authentication is handled through the authentication systems used by ResumeMakery; we do not display your password back to you.

Resume and cover-letter content

You control the content you enter: name, contact details, education, work history, projects, skills, certifications, links, optional photo, template choices and related resume settings. This information can be sensitive because it describes your employment and personal contact details. We use it only to provide the product features you request, including editing, previewing, saving, importing and exporting.

Guest mode

You can build your first resume as a guest. Guest resume data is stored temporarily in browser session storage so a refresh or sign-up handoff does not immediately destroy the draft. A guest draft is not treated as a cloud-saved ResumeMakery account record. If you sign in or create an account through the guest handoff, the current draft can be claimed into your account. Clearing browser/session data may remove an unsaved guest draft.

3. Resume imports and OCR

When you use Upload & Edit, ResumeMakery processes supported PDF, DOCX, text or image input to extract usable resume content. The product is designed to perform parsing and OCR in the browser where possible. Always review imported text because extraction can make mistakes. If a feature or provider changes in a way that sends source files to a server, this policy will be updated before that change is relied on for production use.

4. Analytics and usage measurement

ResumeMakery currently uses web analytics and product-measurement services including Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity and Ahrefs Web Analytics. These services can receive information such as page URL, referrer, browser/device information, approximate location derived from IP address, interaction or session data, and technical identifiers used to measure visits and product performance.

We configure product events so that resume text, names, phone numbers, email addresses and other resume content are not intentionally included in analytics event payloads. However, analytics providers process standard web-request and browser information under their own terms and privacy policies. You can use browser privacy controls, tracking protection, cookie controls or applicable consent settings to limit non-essential measurement where available.

Why we use analytics

  • Understand which public pages and product features are used.
  • Find broken flows, usability problems and performance issues.
  • Measure aggregate acquisition and navigation patterns.
  • Improve mobile layout and product reliability.

5. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

ResumeMakery uses browser storage for authentication, local resume state, settings, security/rate-limit information and guest-draft continuity. Analytics providers may also set or read cookies or similar identifiers depending on your browser, region and consent choices. See the Cookie Policy for more detail.

6. Advertising and Google AdSense

Google AdSense advertising is not currently active on ResumeMakery. ResumeMakery may apply to or enable advertising in the future to support the free service. If advertising is activated, we will update this policy and the Cookie Policy to identify the advertising provider and purposes before serving ads.

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, IP-derived information, device/browser information and advertising identifiers for ad delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, measurement and—where you have provided the required consent—personalised advertising. ResumeMakery will not intentionally send the text of your resume to AdSense for ad targeting.

Where required by law or Google policy, including relevant traffic from the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, we will use an appropriate Google-certified consent management platform before enabling personalised advertising. Users will be offered the consent choices required for their region.

7. Service providers

We use service providers to operate the product. These can include Supabase for authentication/database services, Vercel for hosting and delivery, Google for OAuth and analytics, Microsoft Clarity for product/session analytics and Ahrefs for web analytics. Each provider processes data under its own contractual and privacy terms. We do not sell resume content to these providers or authorise them to use resume text as their own product database.

8. How we use information

  • Authenticate users and maintain account sessions.
  • Build, preview, save, import and export resumes.
  • Provide ATS and resume-quality guidance requested by the user.
  • Protect the service against abuse and troubleshoot failures.
  • Measure aggregate usage and improve the product.
  • Comply with valid legal obligations.

9. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your resume content or personal information to data brokers or advertisers. Data is shared only as needed with service providers that operate ResumeMakery, when you deliberately invoke a connected/optional service, when you consent to a particular use, or when disclosure is legally required.

If advertising is enabled later, ad technology providers may process browser and advertising-related data as disclosed above. That does not give advertisers access to the text of your private ResumeMakery conversation or resume account content.

10. Storage and retention

Guest drafts are temporary browser-session data. Signed-in resume data can be stored locally in the browser and, where cloud sync is enabled, in the ResumeMakery account database. We retain account and saved resume information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service or until it is deleted, subject to backup, security and legal-retention requirements.

11. Security

We use HTTPS and hosting/database security controls intended to protect data in transit and at rest. The application also uses browser security headers and access controls. No internet service can promise absolute security, so users should avoid placing unnecessary sensitive information in a resume and should keep their own exported copy of important documents.

12. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export personal data; withdraw consent; object to or restrict certain processing; or complain to a regulator. ResumeMakery also provides product controls for editing and deleting resume content. To make a privacy request, email privacy@resumemakery.com.

13. Children

ResumeMakery is intended for users aged 16 and above and is not designed as a service for young children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information contrary to this policy, contact us so we can review the request.

14. International processing

Hosting, authentication and analytics providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate provider safeguards and user consent mechanisms for international processing.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the product, analytics, service providers or advertising configuration changes. The date at the top will be updated when material changes are published. If a change requires consent, we will provide the required notice or choice before relying on that processing.

16. Contact

Privacy and data requests: privacy@resumemakery.com
Support: support@resumemakery.com
Legal: legal@resumemakery.com
