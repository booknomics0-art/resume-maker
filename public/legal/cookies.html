Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Summary: ResumeMakery uses necessary browser storage to run account and resume features, and currently uses Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity and Ahrefs Web Analytics to measure public-site/product usage. Google AdSense advertising is not currently active.

1. What we mean by cookies and browser storage

Websites can store small pieces of information in cookies, local storage or session storage. These technologies can keep you signed in, remember a draft, preserve settings, protect against abuse or help measure how a site is used. Some third-party analytics providers may also use identifiers to recognise visits or sessions.

2. Necessary ResumeMakery storage

ResumeMakery uses browser storage for functions such as authentication, local resume state, account/session continuity, settings, security controls and the temporary guest-resume flow. Exact storage keys can change as the product evolves, but their purpose remains to provide the feature requested by the user.

  • Authentication/session storage: keeps a signed-in session working and supports OAuth handoffs.
  • Resume storage: keeps a local copy of saved resume data for fast loading and continuity.
  • Guest draft storage: keeps the current first-resume draft temporarily in the browser session before the user creates an account.
  • Security storage: supports rate limits, audit information or abuse prevention.
  • Optional settings: stores user-selected product preferences.

3. Analytics services currently used

Google Analytics

Google Analytics helps us understand aggregate visits, page views, referral sources, device/browser characteristics and navigation patterns. Google may use cookies or similar technologies depending on browser and consent settings. ResumeMakery does not intentionally include the text of a user's resume, phone number, email address or other resume fields in analytics event payloads.

Microsoft Clarity

Microsoft Clarity helps us find usability problems by providing analytics about page interactions and sessions. Depending on configuration and region, Clarity can use cookies or similar identifiers. We use it to understand interface behaviour, not to create a database of resume content.

Ahrefs Web Analytics

Ahrefs Web Analytics is used to measure web traffic and site performance. It can receive standard web-request and browser information needed for that measurement.

4. Advertising cookies

Google AdSense ads are not currently served on ResumeMakery. If ResumeMakery is approved for and later enables AdSense, advertising cookies or similar identifiers may be used for ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency management and measurement. Personalised advertising will only be enabled where the required user choice or consent has been collected.

Before advertising is activated, this Cookie Policy and the Privacy Policy will remain aligned with the production configuration. Where Google requires a certified consent management platform, including for relevant traffic from the EEA, UK and Switzerland, ResumeMakery will use the required consent flow.

5. Your choices

You can use browser controls to delete or block cookies and site storage. Blocking necessary storage can break sign-in, guest-draft continuity or other core features. Browser tracking-protection tools can also limit analytics and advertising identifiers.

When a consent banner or consent-management interface is presented for your region, use that interface to accept, reject or manage the optional purposes shown there. Necessary storage may still be used when it is required to deliver a feature you requested.

6. Data we do not intentionally send to analytics or advertising systems

ResumeMakery does not intentionally use the text of your private resume, work history, education, phone number, personal email address or uploaded resume file as an analytics or advertising targeting payload. Public page URLs and standard browser/request information may still be processed by measurement or advertising providers as described in this policy and their own policies.

7. Changes

We will update this policy if the production analytics, cookie or advertising configuration changes materially. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest published version.

8. Contact

Questions about cookies or privacy can be sent to privacy@resumemakery.com.
