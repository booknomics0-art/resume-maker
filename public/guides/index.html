How ResumeMakery approaches resume advice

There is no universal resume score that guarantees selection. Different employers, recruiters and applicant tracking systems use different processes. That is why ResumeMakery separates three questions: is the document complete and readable, does it match the language of the target role, and does it contain evidence for the claims it makes?

The strongest improvements are usually specific and verifiable: fixing a missing phone number, rewriting a vague project bullet to show what you built, replacing a generic objective with a role-focused summary, or removing a skill you cannot explain. Repeating keywords without context is rarely a useful substitute for evidence.
